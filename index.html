I work on the compiler for AWS Trainium chips at Annapurna Labs, with a focus on formal verification.

Before AWS, I was a Ph.D. student in the Bristol PL Research Group, advised by Meng Wang and Cristina David. My research was on automatic translation of programs to Rust, combining static analysis, formal methods, and large language models. Before that, I studied at Peking University, where I worked with Zhenjiang Hu on bidirectional programming.
